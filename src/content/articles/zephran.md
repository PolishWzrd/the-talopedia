The **Empire of Zephran** is an Empire at the edge of the continent of **Valtheria**, facing the **Eastern Panthalassan Ocean** to the east, south andwest and the Empire borders the Armed Republic of **Githka** to the North. The Empire has a Population around 34 million people with two major ethnic groups, the *Mistvan* and *Ostvan* which although have shared a rivalry historically, the two groups mixed after the conquest of the 900's. The country is mountinous with long winters, warm spring and cool summer, it's peak's seem to never full melt. It's capital city is named Greyholm after the first royal family that had united the country and created the Duchy of **Ostguard**. The most populous city is **Greyholm**, sitting at around 9.4 million people with the next most populous city of **Ashfelda**, sitting around 3.6 million people.
