The **Empire of Zephran** is an Empire at the edge of the continent of **Valtheria**, facing the **Eastern Panthalassan Ocean** to the east, south andwest and the Empire borders the Armed Republic of **Githka** to the North. The Empire has a Population around 34 million people with two major ethnic groups, the *Mistvan* and *Ostvan* which although have shared a rivalry historically, the two groups mixed after the conquest of the 900's. The country is mountinous with long winters, warm spring and cool summer, it's peak's seem to never full melt. It's capital city is named Greyholm after the first royal family that had united the country and created the Duchy of **Ostguard**. The most populous city is **Greyholm**, sitting at around 9.4 million people with the next most populous city of **Ashfelda**, sitting around 3.6 million people.

## Etymology

The word Zephran derives from the Zephran family that had subjegated the entire state. The word Mistvan derives from the ethnic group that lived mainly in the mountains, which some were in permanant state of being covered by mists and clouds, while the Ostvan were priarily in the plains of Zephran.

## History

The **Zephran Empire** began with it's roots, in 7<sup>th</sup> Century BCE as a loose confederation of local tribes, which was split between two main ethnic groups. The more mountinous **Mistvan**, and the more lowlander **Ostvan**, and these two ethnic groups often warred with each other as the Ostvan arrived later historically. From where it is unkown, as early records contradict each other or have been lost, but from what is known from early records, a smaller tribe started to make a name for itself, which was called the **Ostgard** Tribe. They eventually helped create the Highland Confederacy through alliances or conquest, which the main goal of was to deter foreign invaders and to ease trade and ethnic tensions. The conflicts and diplomacy lasted around a century due to stubborness and resistance as each tribe was fiercly indipendant and often had grevances streching back centuries with other tribes.

The tribes, now after centuries of conflict started to more closely work together and grevances were often settled with duels if the case was accepted by local leaders instead of open conflict. The **Ostguard's** started to spread their influence in their small territory they took and would consolidate their gains and Civilise themselves, and finally founding the **Duchy of Ostguard** in 990 AD. The Duchy started to look outwards on the Petty Kingdos surrounding the state, and the leaders of the Duchy, the **Zephran** family, started to stratigically arry family into the nobility of the Kingdoms and used those alliances to wage war on more hostile states. After 255 years of conquest and alliances, the eventually founded the Zephran Empire in 1245 AD, which still stands until today.

## Government

The Impirial government is an Authoritarian, Semi-Constitutional Monarchy that is led by an **Emperor** or **Empress**, and have two main branches in the **Impirial Assembly**. The **Assembly** hosts two houses, the House of Lord, which is made up largely out of Nobility and the Eclesiarchy who are appointed by the Monarch, while the House of Commons is mainly made out of elected buearocrats that represent all the regions of the Empire.

The current serving **Emperor** is the 25 year old Aleksander Håkon Żółvåg I

who was recently crowned, due to his father passing away due to a heart attack. The Emperor is a reform minded individual who wishes to modernise the nation as well as provide his people with better living standars and education, while improving the economy to better his people and the country long term. He is also unmarried as he was focused on military training as well as having been groomed as the sole heir of the Empire, and he is currently looking for marrige.

## Military

The Zephran military is divided into four main branches, The **Impirial Army**, The **Impirial Air Corps**, The **Impirial Navy**, and the **Special Service Corps**, and are led by the **Impirial High Command** who have full control over the military, while the Emperor or Empress serve as it's head. The state has a 2 year mandatory military service and so they have a large scale pool of reservists if war does break out.

### The Impirial Army

The **Imirial Army** is the backbone of the Impirial military and is led by the **Commisariate**, which are the officer corps of the army, which is subdivided to the **Infantry Corps** and the **Armoured Corps**. The Army is proffesional, although the lack of educated soldiers stops it from being as effective as it could be. Though the Army has a reputation of being 'Unhinged' due to their 'Innovative Tactics', but their tactics which include putting Anti-Tank grenades onto lances and using calvary to flank armoured vechicles and detonate them on their vaulnrable rear to put them out of commision. The **Commisariate** recruit from both nobility and the common people and educate them in both war and command, but also give education to uneducated commoners to help them be more effective officers. The Army recruits both men and women for combat and support roles, and recruit officers from promising recruits who wish to continue serving in the military. The **Mountaineers** are particularly famous among the Impirial Army as they tend to be more unhinged than their unspecialised counterparts, known to plant charges in loose sections of mountains and waiting for columns to getting into positions before blowing the charges and causing landslides before ambushes. They also are very effective when it comes to mountain warfare.

### The Impirial Air Corps

The **Impirial Air Corps** is the smallest military branch of the Empire, they are well trained and well educated to ensure as high of an effectivness as possible. The air force is fighter and close air support focused, with some areial troop transports used for the **Special Service Corps** and their operations, as well as paratroopers to their intended targets. Bombers are more rare as they are mainly used for destroying industry and supporting attacks on frontlines rather than large scale bombings. The air force recruits both men and women into their service.

### The Impirial Navy

The **Impirial Navy** is smaller than some countries but is more focused on submarine warfare and than traditional naval combat. The Empire focuses on convoy raiding as well as having submarines support surface vessles in naval combat as they do not have battleships, as they would be too expensive to maintain with the current budget. Though, the Navy is still proffesional and conduct regular exercises to keep as high proffesionalism as possible and reduce incompetancy.

### The Special Service Corps

The **Special Service Corps** are the special forces of the Empire, with the most well trained and best equiptment the Empire can offer, they specalise in assasination, covert operations, sabotage and information gathering. They also work alongside the Secret Service often when escorting agents and people of high importance withing the Empire and foreign peoples of interest such as diplomats and world leaders. The **Special Service Corps** only recruit those who show the most competance and often refuse those who try to get in due to being simply nobility and often mock them to the chargin of the Nobility, though they are respected. The Service members tend to also be rough around the edges and are the most 'Creative' when it comes to their operations, as they are trained to work with liited recources and often are also described as 'Unprofessionally Proffesional' with their work.

## Economy

The Impirial **Economy** is developing, but they host a great importance on Fishing and Agriculture, Mining, Manufacturing, and Military production. The state has nationalised Mining and Military industry, as well as some Agriculture and Fishing industries, although allows companies to freely operate in a Capitalist economy, though it proritises Interventionism when nessesary. The government also has a Semi-Autarky stance, allowing foreign companies limited contracts and places within the countries to protect it's own industries from potential depressions and companies taking advantage of local industries.

### Infastructure & Education

The government also invests heavily into infastructure to help the economy but also builds homes for their poorest citizens to make them affordable for everyone. They also Invested heavily into Education to help modernise the country, and to start working to make a more educated workforce. But also to help build a more modern military to not lag behind in military industry.

## Geography

The **Zephran** geography is both mountinous and has large forests, plains but lacks any major rivers or lakes. **Zephran** has three major mountain ranges, the **Tatra Mountains**, the **Istvan Heights**, and **Tertian Mountains**, with some of the tallest mountains in the world. Due to this abundance of mountains, mountain climbing is a Polular sport among the Zephran people despite it being dangerous, but many people train to undergo mountaneering with some failies working as porters or guides for generations in the mountains.

The Plains **Zephran** is where many of the Major cities are located, like **Ashfelda**, while some settlment sit in the Valleys of the mountains, with some having Ski-resorts for tourists and other amenaties. Though many mining towns tend to dot the Valleys, with some Monistaries built into the mountains, and having vast complexes carved inside the mountains themselves, which were carved by Monks generation after generation

## Demographics

### Ethnic Groups

The two ethnic groups are the **Ostvan**, that make up around 64.4% of the population and have historically lived in the plains of Zephran, while the **Mistvan**, who live in the more mountinous regions of the Empire, which make up about 38.8% of the populatio. They both have equal rights within the country and hold equal representaion in the country.

### Religeon

There is only a single religeon in the Empire, which is called **Orthodoxy**, which has been around for may hundreds of years. **Orthodoxy** is widly popular and very deeply ingraned into the Empire's culture, it has many saints and is a monothaistic religeon, beliving a singular god, with some military orders that are indipendant but work heavily with the Impirial military.

### Languages

There is one official language, which is the known as the **Impirial Tounge** but there are two recognised dialects, **High Impirial** which is used by the Nobility and Eclesiarchy, and **Common Impirial** which is used by the Common people within the empire. Though the Nobility and the Eclisiarchy can speak the **Common Impirial dialect**.

### Citizenship

To gain Citizenship within the Empire, you have to be within the Empire for 5 years and contribute through work, which can be proven through relavent documents. The documents must have the Impirial Seal or the documents will be considered void and you will have to request new ones with the persons employers signature as proof of work.
